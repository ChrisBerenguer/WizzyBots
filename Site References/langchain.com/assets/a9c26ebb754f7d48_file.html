When and where is Interrupt?

Interrupt 2026 takes place May 13-14 at The Midway, located at 900 Marin St. in San Francisco's Dogpatch neighborhood.

How should I travel to the event?

By air: Fly into San Francisco International Airport (SFO) or Oakland International Airport (OAK)

By rideshare: Uber, Lyft, and Waymo are all readily available. We do not recommend driving your own car. Parking near the venue is limited.

By transit: The venue is a short walk from the 3rd St & Marin St bus and light rail stop

What can I expect at Interrupt?

Interrupt offers two action-packed days, including:
• Keynotes from industry leaders on what's next for agents.
• Real-world lessons from AI teams at Clay, Rippling, Workday, and more about what's actually working in production.
• Hands-on workshops where you'll learn best practices for building, debugging, and shipping reliable agents alongside LangChain product experts.
• Plus plenty of time to connect with fellow builders at our social hour and afterparty.

What does the Interrupt schedule look like?

Registration will begin at 8:00 AM on Day 1 (May 13th), with the LangChain Keynote kicking off at 9:30 AM. We'll wrap up the day with our opening reception at 5:00 PM.

Registration will begin at 9:00 AM on Day 2 (May 14th) with sessions starting at 9:30 AM. We'll wrap up the day of content with an afterparty at 4:00 PM.
