FAQs

FAQs

How much money can I actually earn?

Your commission is based on our net earnings for each subscription. Users are charged on a tax-inclusive basis, so there will be slight variability between net earnings from users in different regions. Learn more about the n8n Cloud pricing here.

How do payouts work?

You need a PayPal account to receive payouts. If you do not already have one, you can sign up for one for free here. We issue payouts once a month, and only on balances of EUR 100 or more. If you receive your payout in another currency, PayPal will set the exchange rate.

Which n8n plan can I promote as an affiliate?

Affiliates can get rewarded for promoting n8n Cloud. You can check out what our plans Starter and Pro include on our pricing page.

What's the difference between the Cloud, Community (self-hosted) and Enterprise licenses?

Our Cloud licenses are perfect for teams getting started with n8n or who only require a limited number of workflows. All licenses include unlimited users and forum support. Plans start from only €20/month, with a 14-day free trial.

For businesses with strict security and performance requirements, our self-hostable Enterprise licenses are the perfect fit. All plans include unlimited workflow executions, unlimited active workflows, unlimited shared products, SSO SAML & LDAP, version control, different environments, and dedicated support with SLA.

Our Community Edition license is a basic, self-hosted version of n8n available for free on GitHub.

Check out our pricing page for more details.

How can I promote my templates as a member of the Creator Hub?

Once your templates are approved, they will be displayed on n8n.io/workflows/. You can locate them by the title you've assigned. After finding your workflow, copy its URL and append your unique affiliate ID to the end for tracking purposes. For instance, the link should look like this: https://n8n.io/workflows/NameOfYourWorkflow/?ref=yourUniqueId&utm_source=affiliate. This ensures that any conversions are properly attributed to you.

Are there any limitations to how I can promote my affiliate link?

We encourage you to use your link only in ways that will benefit potential n8n customers, our community, and the company. We reserve the right to cancel your account at any time due to unethical behavior (e.g. anything which could mislead customers or damage n8n’s brand).

Am I allowed to run paid ad campaigns using my affiliate links?

No, it is not permitted to run any kind of paid ad campaigns as part of your affiliate activity. Any kind of paid ads activity will result in the removal from our affiliate program.
