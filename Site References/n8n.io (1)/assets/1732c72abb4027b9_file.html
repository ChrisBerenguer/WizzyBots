About us

n8n is a workflow automation platform that uniquely combines AI capabilities with business process automation. The platform enables connection to any app or API while maintaining the flexibility of code with the speed of no-code. Released under a fair-code license, n8n can be self-hosted and is supported by a vibrant community of developers and builders. Users can start simple and layer complexity as needed - utilizing the visual builder for quick wins, and adding custom Javascript or Python code where more control is required, enabling the connection of anything to everything. n8n is privately held, with funding from Sequoia, Felicis Ventures, Firstminute Capital, Harpoon Ventures, and others. Individual investors include Eventbrite's Kevin Hartz and Supercell's IIkka Paananen. n8n was founded in 2019 and is headquartered in Berlin.

Workflow Management Software

n8n is a flexible workflow automation tool. With a fair-code distribution model, n8n will always have visible source code, be available to self-host, and allow you to add your own custom functions, logic and integrations. n8n's modular, node-based approach makes it highly versatile, enabling you to connect systems and build powerful AI-infused automations.

    Last Thursday I led an AI enablement session for the n8n GTM team. I won’t pretend it was perfectly polished - half of what I showed was unfinished and I spent the weekend fixing bugs after, but my aim was achieved. The goal of the session wasn’t just to demo what I’d built, but to start a conversation far more interesting than any custom tools/prompts. Mission successful. We spoke about scaling personalisation without losing meaning, avoiding hallucinations when researching accounts, and most importantly - the difference between using these tools to scale volume vs increasing quality of targeted work. Inaccurate research, meaningless personalisation and low quality outreach aren’t new problems to sales teams, but with AI the risk is accelerating them. The companies I see getting the most value from automation aren’t moving the fastest - they are just getting specific about what they want to free up time for. In my world, that’s more quality conversations. In engineering, more roadmap work and less glue scripting. Same principle - scale what’s high impact by automating what isn’t. That is the real conversation worth having right now. Not can AI do, but what can you do with the space it creates.
