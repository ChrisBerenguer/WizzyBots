Why n8n

We’re building something rare at n8n: a product that will transform how the world works - and a culture where collaboration and trust take the place of the usual startup noise.

  • The product

    n8n is becoming the default orchestration layer between AI models and business systems, adopted everywhere from smart home side projects to life-saving UN missions. It has the flexibility engineers demand, the accessibility non-devs deserve and the power to orchestrate the type of multi-agent systems that make everyday builders feel like AI experts.

  • The community

    Our inspiration, our growth engine, and our moat. From day one, builders have been our biggest advocates - and many of our best hires started in the community.

  • The people

    When you ask the team why they join n8n, they’ll start by telling you about the culture: full of open, collaborative and wickedly talented people. People who care about our purpose, our community and each other. Here, titles matter less than ideas. The focus is on solving problems, not climbing ladders. And everyone has a direct line to leadership.

  • The potential

    We’re aiming for extraordinary impact with extraordinary efficiency: $1bn ARR with fewer than 500 people. But this is just a milestone, not the mission; a signal of the extraordinary leverage n8n creates, and a glimpse of how transformative this technology can be.

How we work

Our Operating Principles guide how we show up for our colleagues, our community, and our customers every day. Together, they create a culture of builders who stay hungry, stay brave and stay kind.

Empower others

To each other: This is trust by default. With an experienced and capable team, we give people the space to own decisions and learn in action. We see paralysis as worse than failure, so we foster ownership, autonomy, and courage.

To our community: n8n itself is built to empower others, giving people everywhere the ability to automate, create, and scale their impact. Our purpose is to multiply human potential by making AI orchestration accessible to all.

Integrity

To each other: We choose what’s right over what’s easy. That means being transparent with others, direct in our communication, and honest with ourselves. Self-awareness and accountability aren’t extras - they’re how we earn trust and keep it.

To our community: We do the right thing, even when no one is looking. Open, transparent, and community-driven, n8n is committed to earning trust through security, clarity and accountability.

Speed and efficiency

To each other: We are thoughtful, practical, and we move fast. Process is useful, but only when it helps us build better. We’d rather you ship and learn than wait for permission or perfection.

To our community: We design n8n to deliver outsized efficiency - powerful AI orchestration with less code, less friction, and less overhead. So you can focus on what really matters.

Inspire and think big

To each other: We’re builders at heart. Whether you code, design, sell, or scale, you’ll shape the future by testing, learning, and iterating. Curiosity and pragmatism keep us moving, but it’s our ambition that drives us further, aiming beyond incremental for something truly transformative.

To our community: Our ambition is to prove what’s possible with AI orchestration. We’re building for scale; Excel-level ubiquity that empowers millions to create more than they thought possible.

We’re humble and driven - a community-first team building for the long run, not chasing the typical hype cycle.

Candice

Senior Talent Acquisition Manager

Who thrives here

There’s no one single personality type that thrives here: introverts, extroverts, seasoned experts, serial entrepreneurs and ambitious career-switchers have all found their place.

But most successful colleagues share a builder mindset. That means…

  • You’re curious and hands-on, we’ll even nudge you to build an n8n workflow before applying.
  • You have technical empathy, whatever your role and you’re genuinely passionate about the product itself. You care about how it works, how it feels for users, and how to make it better.
  • You’re genuinely excited by AI and automation not as hyped trends, but as meaningful tools for outcomes.
  • You’re impatient to make things real with a bias for shipping, testing, and learning fast.
  • And you care about the people and community you’re building with and for, seeing customers, contributors, and colleagues as part of the same supportive n8n ecosystem.

Life at n8n

Our drive comes from purpose, not pressure. And we protect that balance with generous benefits and meaningful support:

  • Remote-first, global talent
    Our people work from almost anywhere in Europe, the UK and sometimes the US. We meet at regular offsites to stay connected - because the best talent isn’t all in one place.
  • Regular time off
    We offer a healthy vacation allowance, adjusted by location - because stepping away makes the work (and you) stronger.
  • Learning budget
    Use it for courses, conferences or build weeks - because the best ideas come from staying curious, not standing still.
  • Care is respected
    Whether for kids, family or others who need you - because flexibility is the only way to build something sustainable.
  • Fair pay
    Regular benchmarking keeps compensation fair - because progress only matters if it’s shared.
