Governance & SecurityWith great power comes great controllability

Unlike closed-source platforms, n8n gives you total ownership of your AI & automation workflows with auditable source code and a fully self-hosted option.

Deploy n8n your way, self-hosted or cloud

Run n8n self-hosted or on managed cloud, choosing the deployment model that best fits your security, compliance, and operational requirements.

Provision your users via your IdP

n8n supports SSO, SAML, LDAP, and user provisioning with your existing auth service. And we enforce 2FA. So your team can collaborate securely while keeping compliant with the strictest security standards.

Keep your secret in our own vault

Integrate with 3rd-party secret management tools (HashiCorp Vault, AWS Secrets Manager, Azure Key Vault) to securely fetch and inject your encrypted credentials.

Define fine-grained access rules per project

Protect workflows and data by defining granular and custom project roles, so you can tailor exactly what each user, team, or API is allowed to view, edit, deploy, or manage.

Stream workflow logs to your observability stack

Connect all your workflow events to your observability stack, either through direct integrations or via a webhook to any monitoring solution, so teams can track usage, errors, and changes.

Operate on a SOC 2 audited platform

Your data's security is our commitment. We are SOC 2 audited and regularly perform external pen tests. Visit our Trust Center to learn more about certifications and our security posture.

CollaborationWhere modern DevOps practices meet automation

With n8n, your IT teams can use the CI/CD best practices they know and love. So they can imagine, test, and launch automations faster than ever before.

Scope workflows with unlimited projects

Organize workflows, credentials, and your data into isolated projects to support multiple teams or use cases on the same instance. Projects help you avoid cross-access to data and manage automation at scale with clearer ownership and boundaries.

Safeguard your production environment

With environments you can run development and production environments in isolation safer testing and deployment,and use Git as your single source of truth to track changes, restore previous versions, and collaborate safely on workflows.

Track changes and control deployments

With workflow diffs you can compare the differences between your staging and production environments side by side. So you can catch changes that could break your workflow — before you push to prod.

Performance & MonitoringAI workflows you can trust, when reliability is a must

Moving from PoC to production is daunting when you can’t ensure consistent uptime or accurate results from AI. n8n’s scalable infrastructure and >200 executions per second make shipping your work a smooth experience.

AI Evaluations Build AI Agents that work predictably in prod.

Enterprises need AI agents that behave predictably. n8n evaluations let you test your workflows with real data before deploying, so you know how they’ll perform at scale. Track correctness and speed at every stage, and ensure consistent results across models and updates.

Queue mode & Multi-main Ensure performance at enterprise scale.

Queue mode and multi main instance setups give you the ability to horizontally scale your executions in parallel. So you can prevent servers from crumbling under maxed-out resources by absorbing heavy traffic spikes. And avoid unexpected downtime with automatic failover.

See detailed performance results in our scalability benchmark

Insights Keep a close look at your workflow performances — and ROI.

Insights lets you see executions, failure rate, and time saved at a glance, helping you catch issues early and keep workflows running smoothly. Dive into per-workflow data to optimize performance and demonstrate ROI. When you can show how much time and money n8n saves, getting internal sign-off becomes effortless.
