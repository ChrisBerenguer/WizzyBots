n8n Brand Guidelines

Share the power of automation with our n8n logo kit and its usage guidelines.

Logo

The logo should be used in Brand Black (#040506) over white/light backgrounds, and White (#FFFFFF) over dark backgrounds.

Clear space

Our logo works best when it has enough room to breathe.
Keep a clear space of '4X' around our logo at all times to maintain its visual impact in every composition.

Colors

The n8n logos must always appear as one of n8n’s primary brand colors.
This includes our Pink, Dark and White.

Primary

Pink

#EA4B71

Accent

Dark

#040506

Accent

White

#FFFFFF

Minimum size

To maintain the visual integrity of our logo, don’t scale it below 100 pixels or 30 millimeters wide.

Logo dont's

To keep the n8n brand consistent and recognizable, the logo should always be used as intended. Below are a few examples of incorrect usage to avoid.

Don't use the wordmark alone

Don't align vertically

Don't use shadows

Don't use the logo on a busy background

Don't tilt

Don't squeeze

Don't stretch

Don't reverse the icon and wordmark

Don't change the spacing

Don't change the proportions
